Share files, texts, music and a scratchpad with your devices, peer to peer.

FlipFlop connects your devices directly over the local network, Bluetooth or the internet — share files, paste texts, edit a scratchpad and listen to music together, with no account and no server in the middle.

What it does

Everything syncs directly between members

Each group shares one conflict-free Automerge document synced over encrypted iroh connections, while file bytes travel straight from sender to receiver.

LAN, Internet & Bluetooth

Finds nearby devices automatically via mDNS on the local network and BLE advertisements. Connects anywhere with iroh, and can fall back to Bluetooth LE transport when there is no network at all.

Contacts, Pairing & Groups

Pair with a device in one click or scan its QR code to share a private two-device page. Create multi-device groups and invite contacts; members discover each other through the group and connect directly.

Direct File & Folder Sharing

Offer files, folders, media or installed Android APKs. Bytes stay on your device until fetched, verified by BLAKE3 hash. Supports automatic downloads, resuming interrupted transfers, and optional rehosting.

Texts & Live Scratchpad

Texts is a fast feed for links, codes and clipboard snippets with one-click copy and open. Scratchpad is a shared plain-text document everyone can edit at once with automatic conflict-free merging.

Browser Guest Links

Let anyone join a group from a web browser — no app install required. Gives both a local LAN address (works offline on the same Wi-Fi) and an anywhere link powered by iroh and Automerge compiled to WebAssembly.

Music, Listen Along & Miniapps

Turn on the Music tab to stream songs and album folders on demand without downloading them first. Share what you play and the others listen along, in sync, from wherever the songs live. Extend any group with custom WebAssembly miniapps (checklists, expenses, polls, calendars) that sync state in the group document.

Full-featured Command Line Interface

The flipflop CLI does everything the desktop app does and shares the same device identity — handing commands over a local socket whenever the GUI is already running.

flipflop peers                               # nearby peers and saved contacts
flipflop group new                           # create a group
flipflop file offer G ./photo.jpg            # offer a file to group G
flipflop text send G "https://example.com"   # send a link or snippet
flipflop pad show G                          # read or update the group's scratchpad
flipflop guest new --uploads                 # open a browser guest link
